# The handoff against itself and against the spec

The design handoff, in [`handoff/`](handoff/README.md), has two parts, the screens and the design system, and they differ in places. It also differs in places from `docs/product-spec.md`, which it may not change. This file lists both kinds of difference, with what holds for each. [`direction.md`](direction.md) gives the order by which a difference is settled: the spec for behaviour and for the names of things, a system component over a mock's pixels, the screens for what they draw, the design system for everything else.

The lists were made on 2026-11-26. A reader drew each from the files, and a second reader checked it against the files. The differences between the handoff and what is built are in [`deltas.md`](deltas.md), and the cells of the state matrix that the handoff leaves without a design are in [`handoff-check.md`](handoff-check.md).

## 1. Where the two parts of the handoff differ

The screens against the design system's text and previews. "What holds" is the orchestrator's ruling of 2026-11-26 by the order of `direction.md`, and the owner's to overturn: the screens for what they draw, a system component over either, and the spec over both. A ruling marked for the owner is one where the order alone does not decide, or where it decides against both parts. On 2026-11-26 the owner chose the design system's structure for the iPhone, so for the iPhone's navigation and the placing of its controls the design system holds over the screens, and the rulings that follow from his choice say so.

## 2. Where the handoff differs from the spec

The spec rules. A contradiction is not built. A different name gives way to the spec's word. Something the handoff leaves out is still built, with the system default and a line in `deltas.md`. Something the handoff adds is built only where it is presentation; where it is behaviour, it waits for the owner, who alone changes the spec.
