## Build

- The product on one page, taken from the spec: the promises, the vocabulary, the append window.
- The one fixed brand element: the icon and mark.
- The editing model of `docs/product-spec.md` §7. A note is a stack of segments, each edited on its own, a selection does not cross from one segment into the next, and undo covers the segment being edited. New text enters through the capture field, which is the last element of every open note.
- Every surface to design, by device, with the states each must cover: empty, typing, recording, pending, failed, appending to an open note, search results, conflict.
- `docs/design/brief.md` §6 also holds the states the spec requires: recently deleted, sync status and the account-change prompt, attachments waiting or downloading, a failed segment with its retry, a recording being transcribed on another device, export of all notes, cleanup unavailable, no microphone connected on the Mac, a recording on the Mac started from Siri or Shortcuts, text that could not be saved, a recording that could not start, a watch capture not yet delivered, a recording that stopped at its limit, notes that could not be opened, export progress and its result, a hotkey that another app already uses, the command-line access switch, and the moment a held key waits for the microphone.
- More states that the surfaces must cover:
  - the Live Activity, the Watch and the Mac panel during a hold show whether a recording continues a note or starts a new one, without any text from the note;
  - the cue for each of six events on each device, as `docs/product-spec.md` §4.5 lists them;
  - the six states of a voice segment: saved on the Watch; saved, text on its way; saved, text being made elsewhere; ready; needs you; kept, no text;
  - the stream row, with a note that has no text yet, the largest text sizes and the mark of a late capture;
  - a Search control and widget, and state without text on a widget, control or complication: a recording with its timer, how many captures are waiting, how many failed;
  - the first-run stream, which says that sync is on and may carry up to three dismissible setup rows, and the Settings row that says Live Activities are off;
  - the quiet mark of a segment written through the command line or MCP, and the notice on a segment made by a newer Knot.
- The Watch states show no note content: no stream, no text of a recording and no preview of one. They say nothing about a transcript. The Watch app has one screen, the record control, and no list of recordings. Its complication shows how many recordings wait and how many failed.
- Both branches of the three surfaces a spike still decides: a recording from a locked iPhone or only after Face ID; a Watch start with or without the app in front; and the Mac's first run with or without a permission prompt for the hotkey.
- The state matrix. The matrix that ends `docs/design/brief.md` §6 has 49 states by six surfaces. It has the row "A segment moved to its own note, with undo". WP-21's preview test reads the matrix from the brief's own table, so there is no second list.
- The interaction questions the designer should answer:
  - how the stream and the capture field relate on iPhone;
  - how the note being appended to appears in the capture surface;
  - how segments of different kinds read as one note;
  - the Mac panel while typing and while recording;
  - the recording Live Activity and Dynamic Island;
  - the watch recording screen;
  - how typing and Pencil share the iPad capture surface;
  - where a lasting problem shows outside Settings.
- Constraints: system materials, light and dark, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, SF Symbols, touch targets at 44 points or more, and exact device sizes (iPhone 17 Pro, iPad mini, the Mac panel and window, Watch).
- Where a mock and a system component differ, the system component wins. Type maps to Dynamic Type text styles, colour to roles and materials to system materials. Controls, complications, widgets and the Live Activity take glyphs, strings and content from the handoff and rendering from the system.
- The direction step, described in `docs/design/brief.md` §9. It asks for directions for the iPhone capture surface and the Mac panel, and scores each direction on three tasks counted in actions: capture a new thought, continue the last note, find an old one.
- The handoff format wanted: tokens, a component inventory, per-screen specs and motion.

## Failure rules

- The design may propose another editing model in the direction step, and the owner must accept it before code depends on it.
- The full design round does not wait for the spikes, because the brief asks for both branches of each surface a spike still decides.
