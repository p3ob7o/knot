# Open points of the fold

This file lists what the fold could not settle. While they rewrote the plan as `PLAN.md` and the briefs, the writers noted each place where the plan was silent, contradictory or out of date, and the auditor added notes of its own. Those notes are merged here into one numbered list, and each point was checked against the folded documents as they stand on 2026-11-19.

The file adds no requirement and changes no rule. It is for the orchestrator and the owner to work through before the packages concerned start. A point is closed by a decision recorded where it belongs: in a brief, in `PLAN.md`, in `docs/architecture.md` or in `docs/product-spec.md`. The point is then removed from this file.

A second-model review then read the fold item by item, and the points it settled are gone from the list: who owns `docs/plan/`, where `AppEnvironment` is first declared, which thread runs WP-02, who builds the Mac's About actions, what the MCP server does while access is off, and which PR embeds `knot`. The orchestrator has since settled three more: a crash host for KnotInk, who commits the evaluation results, and where a short note goes. The reviews of the first two packages settled who owns the crash hosts, the `knot-eval` target and `Package.resolved`. Before wave 1 the orchestrator settled eleven more: how a spike's report changes the architecture, the branch and the report of SP-5, the pull requests of WP-01 and WP-02 with the paths and the named tests the fold gave them, where the owner finds the guard's table, the two readings of the timed targets, the milestones of two measurements, the cleanup's OS build in §4, what §18 says of three scripts, and two rows of the owner's calendar. After the first code merged, the orchestrator settled four more: who sets `arrivedAt`, the reading of the named test that WP-05, WP-11 and WP-22 share, who records what made a transcript, and who owns the capture module's test folders. After the notes of SP-1, SP-2 and WP-18, the orchestrator settled five more: what SP-4's short note carries of CloudKit's record limit, what export does with a file that is on another device, that the PRs of WP-17, WP-19 and WP-34 stand as their briefs split them, who implements the seven supporting ports, which settles the half on WP-15 of the point on start rows, because the port of background time comes with WP-02's second PR, and how a drawing renders and exports before WP-18 merges. On 2026-11-20 the owner settled whether a speech or cleanup pull request waits for his recordings: it does not, and AGENTS.md says so. Before wave 2 the orchestrator settled five more: what the first-run line on sync shows in each sync state, with the wording for sync switched off left to the owner as a point of its own below; that WP-16's PRs are named when SP-4's report has merged; the two lines of the first draft in WP-11 and WP-33; the split of the rules on who processes a recording; and the PRs of the UI packages, as their briefs split them. The orchestrator then settled who owns the tests of the `knot` executable. After the first merges of wave 2 the orchestrator confirmed three points as the briefs have them, apart from what pull request 52 changed for WP-11: the start rows read by the last-PR rule, the PRs of the wave 2 modules, and the owners of the named tests of the later packages. The contract pull request 54 settled how a test that needs the generated audio runs: it looks up its fixtures before it asks for a model, a missing fixture fails it, and only a missing model skips it.

Each section puts first the points that block the earliest packages. Item IDs such as `eng-071` are those of `docs/plan/amendments.md`.

## Rules that pull against each other

6. **The files of an unreadable inbox item.** An inbox item that cannot be read, or that a newer version wrote, becomes a failed segment and keeps its files in `Inbox/`: the store records their names, and a screen reads the names to open or share each file (docs/architecture.md §6, rule 2). Nothing in `Inbox/` is deleted before it is stored, so the files stay, and every scan meets them again. No rule says what removes the files once the person has deleted that segment. The owner decides it, and the orchestrator writes his answer into §6 before WP-15's first PR.

7. **One applier of the press rule outside KnotUI.** The rule for a press has two appliers on `main`, `NoteViewModel.press` in KnotUI and `WatchCoordinator` in KnotLink. An intent and the Mac's hotkey have no view model, so the rule needs one applier outside KnotUI for the button, the intents and the hotkey. The orchestrator ruled on 2026-11-21 that the capture engine applies it where there is an engine, through an operation of its port, and that the Watch's coordinator stays the Watch's applier. A contract pull request on the engine's port carries the ruling, after pull request 58 has merged, and this point is removed when it merges.

8. **Two places for the recording state.** docs/architecture.md §5 and §13 and the `RecordingStateStore` port keep the recording state in the app group's defaults, which SP-1 found to fit. §7 and `StoreLocations.recordingState`, a folder that nothing uses, put it in a place in the group's container. The orchestrator ruled on 2026-11-21 that the defaults stand, and that the composition gives them. A contract pull request removes the folder or gives it a use, before WP-22 PR 2 starts.

9. **The composition during a restore.** A restore takes in what other writers put below the store's root while it runs (docs/architecture.md §7). No document says whether the composition holds captures while a restore runs, or leaves it to the store. The orchestrator settles it before WP-22 PR 2 starts.

10. **A start row that misses a dependency.** WP-31's second PR holds the Sketch control, which opens the canvas through the OpenSketch intent of WP-19's fourth PR, while its row names only the ruling (`eng-071`). The brief keeps the plan's row and adds a sentence that OpenSketch is held too. The orchestrator settles the row.

11. **Settings lines from the system surfaces.** WP-31's first PR builds "the Action Button tip in Settings", but Settings is in `Apps/iOS/`, which WP-30 owns, and WP-31 owns only `Apps/Extensions/Widgets-iOS/` (`eng-070`). `design-19` names no builder for the first-run stream rows or the Settings line saying that Live Activities are off. 12. **The intents table.** The table in docs/architecture.md §14 marks StopRecording "iPhone and iPad only", lists Siri and Shortcuts among ToggleRecording's users, and has no row for StartRecording on iPhone and iPad (`dx-20`). The orchestrator settles them with the table after SP-1's report, and before WP-19's second PR.

If the second step is cut, WP-40d waits in the same way for WP-32's third PR (`eng-057`). PLAN.md §5 also lists WP-32's third PR among what M6 needs, and WP-31's second PR if M5 is cut. PLAN.md §4 has the orchestrator update the briefs when a part is cut, and no text says what those sub-packages, or M6, then wait for. The orchestrator settles it at the cut.

## Documents that lack what the plan says of them

22. **Full iCloud storage.** `eng-099` says that when iCloud storage is full, sync says so, and text waits with the recordings while everything stays on the device. WP-16 and WP-21 carry the rule, but neither docs/architecture.md §11 nor docs/product-spec.md §9 does, although PLAN.md §7 says failure behaviour is fixed in docs/architecture.md. The orchestrator settles it before WP-21's second PR and WP-16.

23. **The thresholds that left WP-10 and WP-30.** `eng-031` takes the time thresholds out of the merge criteria of WP-10 and WP-30, and WP-42's brief now names them: with 10,000 notes on the Mac, a stream page against 16 ms and a search against 50 ms, and the iPhone's launch against its warm target. docs/product-spec.md §14 has rows for the search and the launch and none for the stream page. The orchestrator settles whether §14 gains that row before M1, the first milestone at which `--perf` measures.

24. **Where an open drawing's saves go.** `CaptureEngine` publishes the revision of the content that is durably saved, and its comment says that a drawing's saves are not in the contract yet. `CaptureCheckpoint` carries text only, and an open drawing has no body in the store, so no operation of `NoteStore` makes the strokes of an open drawing durable. The ink types come with WP-02's third PR. It is settled there or with WP-18, before the first pull request that builds the canvas. WP-11's fourth pull request waits for it, and the engine builds nothing for drawings before.

25. **A privacy manifest for a bare tool.** docs/architecture.md §19 asks for a required reason for each API that needs one, in every target. The `knot` tool is a bare executable: it has no `Resources` folder for a manifest, and it links GRDB statically, whose manifest sits in a bundle the tool does not carry. SP-3's spike app carried no manifest in its bare tool and did not try to embed one (docs/notes/SP-3.md). §19 does not say whether a bare executable carries a manifest of its own, or, if it cannot, whether its reasons go in the app's manifest. The upload of the distribution-signed check shows whether App Store Connect accepts the app's manifest for the tool. The orchestrator settles §19 with SP-3's report.

## Contracts that lack what the packages need

26. **A capture carries the capture it follows.** `NewCapture` has no link to the capture it follows, so a handoff whose first half was "New note", or a named note past the window, can land in two notes when the first half's start is held or falls back to `Inbox/` (docs/architecture.md §20). A contract pull request gives `NewCapture` the link, and `record` and `ingest` place a follower in the note of the capture it follows, also when that capture's row or item comes after the follower's. The orchestrator arranges it before WP-11 PR 2 starts, which waits for it.

27. **Where the level peaks go when a capture ends.** docs/architecture.md §8 says that a stored recording keeps a small array of level peaks beside it. No document says where the peaks go when the capture ends, so that a stored recording keeps them: on the recording's row or as a file beside the asset, and how the Watch's outbox, sync and a view model reach them. It is a gap against §8 that must close before the M2 build. The orchestrator ruled on 2026-11-21: while a recording is made the recorder writes them to a file beside the working file, in a format that KnotCore holds, and when the capture ends they are kept on the voice segment's row, set and read by two operations of the store, and synced as a field of the segment's record. Two contract pull requests carry the ruling, and this point is removed when the second has merged. How the peaks of a Watch recording reach the iPhone stays open until WP-17's second pull request.

28. **One policy for what a test's or an agent's process may not do.** The recorder refuses the hardware input and the live speech system refuses an install, each by its own copy of the same signs: a simulator, a test bundle or runner, `KNOT_AGENT=1` (docs/architecture.md §19). A contract pull request puts the signs in one place in KnotCore and says what each package refuses, with the one launcher of the UI tests in `Apps/UITestSupport/`. The launcher comes with WP-22 PR 1, which makes the composition live. The orchestrator arranges the contract pull request before WP-22 PR 2 starts.

29. **Live words and levels from the engine.** The capture surface shows live words and levels while a recording runs (docs/product-spec.md §4), and `CaptureEngine` publishes neither. WP-21's first PR found the gap. The orchestrator ruled on 2026-11-21, on a note, that the engine publishes them through an operation of its port, for the screen only, and starts live text with the first reader. A contract pull request on the engine's port carries the ruling, after pull request 58 has merged, and it merges before WP-21's second PR starts. WP-11 builds to it.

30. **Why a recording ended, after a relaunch.** A recording ends with its reason, but `CaptureManifest` has no field for it, so the reason is not kept across a relaunch. WP-12's first pull request, pull request 57, found it. The orchestrator settles it with the screens that show an ended recording, before the first pull request that builds one starts.

31. **Taking over the work of a recording.** "Move the work to this device" takes over a voice segment's `processingOwner`, and `NoteStore` has no operation that does it. WP-21's first PR found the gap. The rule on who processes a recording is WP-16's, and the queue that runs the work is WP-15's. The orchestrator prepares a contract pull request with a note first, with WP-15 and WP-16, before the package that needs the operation starts.

35. **Which words are fillers.** WP-14's proof step treats only sounds as fillers: um, uh and the like. It leaves "like", "you know" and "I mean" as words, because they often carry meaning, so a cleanup that removes them is refused and the raw transcript stays (docs/notes/WP-14.md). He decides at the cleanup sitting of G4 whether they ever count as fillers.

36. **A segment whose save failed.** The table of product spec §5 says that "Needs you" means that a step failed, and it offers Retry. A typed capture or a drawing whose save failed is shown as "Needs you" too, with its content kept, and Knot stores it by itself when it can: nothing a person does retries it. So the state promises an action that this case does not have. He decides at the design round, G2, whether such a segment offers Retry or shows a state of its own. `docs/design/brief.md` already lists text that could not be saved among the states to design.

37. **A conflict copy in an export.** An export should mark a conflict copy with the words the app uses for it, and those words come from the design round, G2. Until then a conflict copy leaves in an export with no marker. He decides the words at G2. The marker then goes into product spec §8 and WP-20's brief, and this point is removed.

38. **The first-run line when sync is switched off.** The empty stream on first run says that sync is on and where its switch is (docs/product-spec.md §12). WP-21's view model carries three cases: sync is on, sync is off by the person's own switch, and sync is not available in this build. With sync not available it says nothing about sync. No document gives the words for the case where the person has switched sync off, and until one does the stream shows no line about sync there. He decides the words with the design brief, at G2. They then go into product spec §12 and WP-21's brief, and this point is removed.

39. **A typed capture that ends under its surface.** A typed capture ends when a recording or another surface starts (product spec §3, rules 13 and 15). What it saved is in its note, and text the field holds beyond it stays in the field and begins a capture of its own (docs/plan/WP-21.md). No document says how the surface tells the person that the earlier text was saved. He decides at the design round, G2, before WP-21's second PR starts.
