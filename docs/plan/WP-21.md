## Build

### PR 1

- The first PR is a skeleton that does not depend on the design direction.
- The theme layer, with placeholder values. `Packages/KnotKit/Sources/KnotUI/Theme/` holds colour roles, the type ramp, spacing, radii and motion. WP-40 replaces the values from the handoff.
- View models for the stream, a note, capture, search and settings. View models hold the behaviour and are tested without views. Views stay thin, so restyling does not touch logic. Views never touch storage: they talk to view models, and view models talk to ports.
- Components: the stream row, the three segment views with their states, the capture field, record button, waveform, audio player, pending and failed markers, search field and settings forms.
- VoiceOver labels and Dynamic Type from the first PR.
- Accessibility is behaviour, as docs/product-spec.md §13 states it. WP-21 builds it into its components: VoiceOver announces the start and stop of a recording and stays silent while it runs; new text never moves focus; focus returns when a surface closes; every segment action is reachable from the keyboard on the Mac and the iPad.
- The system's accessibility audit runs in every UI smoke test from this PR on. Exclusions sit in one file, each with a reason (docs/architecture.md §17). WP-41 makes the audit clean on every designed cell.
- The previews live in KnotUIPreviews and run on `AppEnvironment.preview`: the fakes, with the real capture engine and queue over them on iOS and macOS.
- The editing model, fixed in docs/product-spec.md §7 as assumption A27. A note is a stack of segments, each edited on its own, and undo covers the segment being edited. New text enters through the capture field, which is the last element of every open note. With the append window open, the capture surface is that note with its field focused, so WP-21 builds one view model for the note and for capture.
- A selection of segments. Several segments can be selected at once, as whole segments, and copied, shared or deleted together, as docs/product-spec.md §7 and §8 state. A selection is copied or shared in the order of the note. A text selection made inside a segment stays inside it. WP-21 adds the selection and its three actions to the note's view model, with tests for the order of the copied text and for one undo. "Move to its own note" still takes one segment at a time, and moving several selected segments together into one new note is left out.
- The capture surface follows docs/product-spec.md §4.1. It shows the note a capture would join, following the window, and the destination is fixed at the first keystroke. When the window runs out while the field is still empty, the earlier note leaves the surface, and a view-model test covers that case.
- The append rule has one owner. The note a capture surface shows comes from a store query that calls the same rule as the store's record-a-capture transaction. The view model does not apply the rule itself.
- "New note" is one action away wherever the capture surface shows the note: the iPhone and iPad capture surface, the Mac panel and an open note (rule 4 of docs/product-spec.md §3).
- The six states of a voice segment, named in docs/product-spec.md §5 by what the user can do: saved on the Watch; saved, text on its way; saved, text being made elsewhere; ready; needs you; kept, no text. The mapping from internal states to the six, and the settled rule, live in KnotCore and are built by WP-02. KnotUI uses both. A view-model test covers each of the six states. Retry on another device is labelled as moving the work to this device.
- The stream row follows docs/product-spec.md §7. Its preview is the latest segment's text. A note with no text says what it holds. At large text sizes the preview gives way first, then the kinds. The title or first line, the time and a failed marker always stay. A late capture is marked as arrived until its note is opened, and the stream says once that captures arrived.
- "Move to its own note", as docs/product-spec.md §7 and rule 16 of §3 state, with assumption A37. WP-21 adds the action and its undo to the note's view model. The action sits beside Delete on every segment of a note that holds another live segment. The app undoes it at once, and the window follows the most recent capture into its new note. The app's undo is the store's return. Tests cover when the action is offered and an undo after the note it left was deleted.
- A segment written through the command line or MCP carries a quiet mark. A segment made by a newer Knot says so and asks for an update.
- An open note that is deleted on another device says so and offers to restore it (docs/product-spec.md §7).
- A recording or drawing that was purged after a restored copy was made shows as missing, never as a broken player (docs/architecture.md §7).
- The first-run stream says that sync is on and where its switch is. It may also carry up to three dismissible rows on setting up the Action Button, a Lock Screen control and the Watch complication (assumption A29). If Live Activities are off, Settings says that recording from the Lock Screen will open Knot.
- When the store is unavailable, captures go to `Inbox/` and the stream says what is wrong.

### PR 2

- The second PR builds from the structure sheets in `docs/design/direction.md`: for the capture surface of each of the four devices, its regions, its navigation, and the component in each state.
- The ink segment view and its states, on the ink types of WP-02 PR 3: shown, being recognised, failed, nothing recognised.
- Sync status in the settings view model: off, syncing, error, iCloud storage full, no account. When iCloud storage is full, sync says so, and text waits with the recordings while everything stays on the device.
- The state matrix. docs/design/brief.md §6 ends with a matrix of 49 states by six surfaces. WP-21 adds one named preview in KnotUIPreviews for every cell marked as designed, and a test fails when a cell has none. The test reads the state matrix from the brief's own table, so there is no second list. The matrix holds the rows "A segment moved to its own note, with undo" and "Note, several segments selected".
- WP-40's side-by-side screenshots are rendered from those previews.

## Failure rules

- If the design handoff arrives late or changes structure, the cost is UI rework. Behaviour lives in view models. Views are thin and restyled in WP-40.
- The design may propose another editing model in the direction step, and the owner must accept it before code depends on it.
- The selection rests on a reading that waits for the owner, assumption A39 of docs/product-spec.md §16: "select multiple segments" means whole segments, with copy, share and delete. The other reading is a text selection that runs from one segment into the next, which needs the rich-text editor that D20 rules out.
- A return is refused when the segment's note has changed since the move, or when the note it left is deleted or gone. It then changes nothing.
- The store decides where a capture goes. The capture surface only shows the store's prediction, and the store's transaction makes the decision.
- When the store is unavailable, capture still works and the stream says what is wrong.

## Named tests

- `accessibility-audit-in-every-ui-smoke-test`: Every UI smoke test runs the system's accessibility audit from WP-21's first PR.
- `preview-for-every-designed-matrix-cell`: KnotUIPreviews holds one named preview for every cell of the state matrix marked as designed, and a test that reads that matrix from the brief's own table fails when a cell has none.
- `capture-window-runs-out-while-field-empty`: A view-model test covers the window running out while the field is empty.
- `voice-segment-six-states-view-model`: A view-model test for each of the six states of a voice segment.
- `move-action-when-offered`: WP-21 adds the move action and its undo to the note's view model, with a test for when the action is offered.
- `move-undo-after-note-it-left-deleted`: WP-21 adds the move action and its undo to the note's view model, with a test for an undo after the note it left was deleted.
- `selection-order-of-copied-text`: WP-21 adds the selection and its three actions to the note's view model, with a test for the order of the copied text.
- `selection-one-undo`: WP-21 adds the selection and its three actions to the note's view model, with a test for one undo.
