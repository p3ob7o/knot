# Where each amendment went

The plan was approved on 2026-11-19 as a first draft followed by five lists of amendments: one from each of the four reviews, and one with the owner's answers on the assumptions of the product spec. The fold (WP-00, pull request 2) turned that into one text: [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md) and one brief for each package in this folder.

This table has one row for each item of the five lists, by list and position. It says what the item asks, where its content lives now, and what earlier text it replaced. The lists themselves are kept word for word in [docs/reviews/2026-11-19-plan-review.md](../reviews/2026-11-19-plan-review.md).

How to read it:

- An item is named by its list and its position: `eng-071` is the 71st item of the engineering list.
- Order of precedence: the first draft, then `ceo`, `design`, `dx`, `eng` and `owner`. Inside a list, a later item wins.
- "Where it went" names sections of PLAN.md, briefs by package, and the other documents that carry the item. A document named there already carried the item's content before the fold.
- "What it replaced" quotes the earlier text in “quotation marks”, with where it stood. `scripts/check-plan.sh` fails when one of those texts is back in PLAN.md or in a brief, and when an item has no row or more than one.
