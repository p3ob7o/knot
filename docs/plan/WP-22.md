### PR 1

- `AppEnvironment.live`. WP-22 builds the live environment for iOS, iPadOS and macOS only, and WP-32 composes the watch app.
- The factory. `AppEnvironment` is declared in KnotCore with an `.unavailable` value. WP-01 writes the environment factory in KnotApp so that a debug build asks KnotFakes for the preview environment. The factory returns `.live` from the first PR of WP-22, with any port not yet implemented filled by the unavailable one. - Each module exposes one live entry point that returns the unavailable port until its package fills it in, so WP-16, WP-17, WP-18 and WP-19 wire themselves in without editing `Packages/KnotKit/Sources/KnotApp/`.
- The allowed list of unavailable ports per milestone, with the test that checks `.live` against it.
- The launch sequence. The capture surface is on screen before the launch steps run. The steps are: open the store, recover recordings, ingest the inbox, finish the open captures of earlier launches, resume jobs, start sync if it is on. Ingest runs before recovery ends the open rows (docs/architecture.md §5).
- Launch steps depend on one another as docs/architecture.md §13 states: the steps that need the store are skipped when it did not open, jobs and sync wait for recovery and ingest, and a step in which the last two launches died is skipped and reported.
- Crash recovery covers all three kinds. A typed draft found at relaunch becomes a finished segment, a drawing found at relaunch goes on to recognition, and a recovered capture ends at its last durable write. The step that finishes the open captures of earlier launches comes after ingest. It does not sit beside the step that recovers recordings.
- When the store is unavailable, captures go to `Inbox/` and the stream says what is wrong.
- The engine never waits for the store. A capture that begins while the store is not open, is migrating or has not answered within 100 ms goes to `Inbox/` in the manifest format and is ingested later. WP-22 has a named test for each case.
- Signposts for every target in docs/product-spec.md §14.
- The diagnostics exporter. Test builds export a diagnostics file from About: capture rows, job rows, counts, signpost timings, the build and the OS, with no note text, titles or content-derived names. WP-22 builds the exporter, and WP-30 and WP-33 the entry in About.
- The keep-gate counts. The exporter carries the week's counts without any note text: captures a day by kind, captures that failed or stayed pending, uses of "New note", and segments moved to their own note. It reads the table of daily counters that WP-10 keeps, as docs/architecture.md §7 and §19 state.
- Milestone levels. Every milestone build, and every fix built for an earlier milestone, is made at a level from M1 to M7, and a feature whose milestone has not passed is switched off in it by a gate: sync and the command line open at M4, and ink at M5. WP-01 builds the mechanism, and WP-22 the test that lists the gates for each level.
- A package test of the composition: type, drop the environment, build it again, run the launch sequence, read.
- The UI smoke test on `.live`, as docs/architecture.md §17 states it: the app on `.live` with a temporary store, type, kill, relaunch, read. The first PRs of WP-30 and WP-33 carry it on the iPhone and the Mac, and they merge after WP-22 PR 1. The named test `live-smoke-type-kill-relaunch-read-composition` comes with WP-22 PR 2. ### WP-22c

- A small pull request after the end-to-end suite, by the orchestrator's ruling of 2026-11-23 on what the composition does during a restore, which no document said: the composition's restore of a kept copy, in the simpler of the two forms weighed.
- `LiveComposition.restore(_:)` ends the launch's steps first, closes the store's gate, holds background time on iOS, closes the store's pool and then calls the store's own restore, `StoreCopies.restore`, off the cooperative pool.
- After a restore the store stays closed for the rest of the launch. Every call of the store's ports fails with `store_not_open`, a capture takes `Inbox/`, and the composition says that Knot must be opened again, as a state a view shows. The next launch opens the restored store and runs its steps as any launch does. Nothing of a launch runs twice in one launch, and a second restore in the same launch is refused.
- A capture open across the restore is not lost: a typed line begun before it and ended after it, and a recording begun before it and stopped after it, are each whole and there once after the next launch. The recording's end reports `store_not_open` until then.
- A known limit: a store call that passed the gate before it closed and reaches the pool after the pool closed fails with `unexpected`, not `store_not_open`. The capture falls back to `Inbox/` with that code and is stored once at the next launch. A barrier over every forwarded call was weighed and left out.
- The control that starts a restore and the words for the state are the apps' packages': WP-30a and WP-33 PR 2.

### WP-22f

- A small pull request beside those this package planned, by the orchestrator's ruling of 2026-11-28, to supply the composition's export of the store as it is (docs/architecture.md §7; docs/product-spec.md §11) for About in both WP-30 and WP-33 PR 2.
- `LiveComposition.exportStore(into:)` (and `AppComposition.exportStore(into:)`) runs off Swift's cooperative pool on a dedicated thread while holding background time on iOS, without calling the launch sequence or closing the store.
- When the store is open, it writes a checked, WAL-free SQLite backup through `StoreDatabase.writeCopy(to:)` (allowed by hand in `Packages/KnotKit/Sources/KnotStore/Database/StoreCopies.swift`, rejecting occupied destinations and sidecars and publishing without replace) and copies the rest of the store folder recursively, preserving `.claim` symlinks and skipping disappearing hidden partial files.
- When the store cannot open, is still opening, or was closed for restore, it never opens or modifies SQLite: it copies the store folder and its SQLite journals verbatim as-is, failing and removing its partial output if a listed entry disappears mid-copy. In both modes, a separate app-group `Inbox/` is copied alongside when present.

## Failure rules

- The capture surface is on screen before the launch steps run.
- A step that needs the store is skipped when the store did not open. Jobs and sync wait for recovery and ingest.
- A step in which the last two launches died is skipped and reported.
- A capture belongs to the process launch that began it, and recovery finishes only the captures of earlier launches.
- When the store is unavailable, captures go to `Inbox/` and the stream says what is wrong.
- The diagnostics file holds no note text, no titles and no content-derived names. The content hash of a capture stays out of it.
- `StoreLocations` in KnotCore is the only code that computes a store path, with no default in any library target. That holds for KnotApp.
- A fix made for M2 during the keep-gate week carries nothing from M3 or M4.
- A soak failure is never retried to green. It prints its seed, its kill point and where the store was kept.
