## Failure rules

- A recording or a drawing may still be on another device. docs/product-spec.md §8 and §10 say what export does then. The app's export first downloads them and lists those it could not get. `knot export` never downloads: it refuses when attachments are on another device, and `--skip-missing` exports the rest with a list.
- A sandboxed tool probably cannot write to a folder given as an argument, so SP-3 decides how `knot export` delivers its output (docs/architecture.md §15).
- Importing an export is deferred to `TODOS.md`.
