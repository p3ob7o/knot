## Build

- Audio fixtures generated by a script from text, using the system voices: clean speech, speech with scripted fillers and self-corrections, a list, numbers and names, silence, a 10-minute file and a noisy file. Each has a reference transcript.
- WP-04 says whether the synthetic audio is committed or generated, where tests find it, and the error a test gives when it is absent.
- A cleanup evaluation set: 30 raw transcripts, each with notes on what a correct cleanup keeps and removes.
- The cleanup evaluation set includes at least ten meaning-critical cases: negations, amounts, dates, names and spoken self-corrections. The evaluation reports them separately. The meaning-critical set also has cases in which only the punctuation differs. In v1 a correct cleanup leaves spoken self-corrections as they were said and leaves numbers untouched.
- The synthetic cleanup and speech results are committed with the fixtures.
- Ink fixtures built from Bézier paths: a line of handwriting, a list and a sketch.
- A read-aloud script of 10 short English passages with known text, and a request for 10 spontaneous voice notes. The owner records both with Apple's Voice Memos, on the iPhone and on the Watch, in wave 0, so that the proof steps of WP-13 and WP-14 run on them.
- The owner puts those files in the folder named by `KNOT_LOCAL_FIXTURES`, named `read-aloud-<number>-<device>.m4a` and `note-<number>-<device>.m4a`. They stay on the build Mac: they are recordings he makes for the build and chooses himself, not notes from his stream.
- One checklist per milestone, M1 to M7. Each row is one action, its expected result, and a pass or fail box. A checklist longer than 20 rows is split into two sittings.
- Each milestone is something Paolo can do on a device, and its checklist proves it:

  | ID | Name | Paolo can |
  |---|---|---|
  | M1 | Skeleton on four devices | Type a line on iPhone, iPad and Mac and find it in the stream after relaunch. Record on the Watch and see the capture listed on the Watch as saved. Local only, system-default look. |
  | M2 | iPhone voice and text | Press the Action Button, speak, and find a cleaned, titled note. Type notes, watch the append window work, search, edit, delete and undo. Kill the app while recording and lose nothing. |

  | M5 | iPad Pencil | Write by hand, search the handwriting, copy it as text, and see the drawing on the Mac and iPhone. |

  | M7 | TestFlight v1 | Install Knot from TestFlight on every device. |

## Failure rules

- Paolo's own recordings go in the folder named by `KNOT_LOCAL_FIXTURES` and are never committed. Nothing derived from them enters a commit, a PR, a log or a test. They are the one exemption from the synthetic-fixtures rule, and examples shown in PRs stay synthetic.
- One changed meaning among the meaning-critical cases sends the cleanup default to the owner's decision.
- A meaning-critical case that passed and now fails blocks the PR.
