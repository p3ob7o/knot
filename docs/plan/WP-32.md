## Build

### PR 1

- The first PR is a skeleton that does not depend on the design direction.
- WP-32 composes the watch app. WP-22 builds the live environment for iOS, iPadOS and macOS only. The watch app does not use `CaptureEngine` and has no database: it composes KnotAudio, KnotLink and KnotUI itself.
- The watch app hosts the coordinator of WP-17 PR 1, which records, converts and sends, beside the outbox in KnotLink.
- The owner records on the Watch and sees the capture listed on the Watch as saved. M1 adds no playback on the Watch, and delivery to the iPhone belongs to M3.

### PR 2

- The second PR builds from the Watch's structure sheet in `docs/design/direction.md`.
- The recording screen: one control, Double Tap as its primary action, haptics, a timer and a waveform.
- Recording with the wrist down.
- Recent recordings. The Watch lists its own recent recordings, each with a time, a length and a delivery state: it still waits for the iPhone, it has been sent, or it could not be delivered. It says nothing about a transcript.
- The indicator. During a recording the Watch shows whether the recording continues a note or starts a new one, without any text from the note. WP-17's back channel carries only what that needs: the end of the capture that a new recording would join, if there is one, and the window.
- The cues. docs/product-spec.md §4.5 lists the cue for each of six events on each device, and WP-32 implements those of the Watch. When a capture is delivered to the iPhone, its row changes to sent. Until the "live" cue, nothing implies that Knot is recording.
- The Watch's StartRecording opens the watch app recording and calls the watch recorder, not `CaptureEngine`. Intents reach the environment through the App Intents dependency manager, which the app registers at launch.
- The first complication family, and a control for Control Center and the Ultra's Action Button.
- A complication or control may show state without text: a recording with its timer, how many captures are waiting, how many failed.
- The cells of the state matrix in docs/design/brief.md §6 that this package implements are those of the Watch column marked "Yes":
  - Recording, live
  - Recording, continues a note or starts a new one
  - Recording, display dimmed
  - Just stopped: saved, with text arriving except on the Watch
  - Short recording discarded
  - Stopped at the limit, or by a call
  - Could not start, with the reason
  - Recent recordings: waiting, sent, delivery failed
  - Storage nearly full
  - Launchers: idle, recording, waiting, failed. PR 2 holds the first complication family and the control, and PR 3 the rest.

## Failure rules

- The Watch is a capture device only. It shows no note content: no stream, no text of a recording and no preview of one, and there is no typing on it (docs/product-spec.md §6).
- The indicator is a forecast. The phone decides at ingest, by the recording's own timestamps (docs/architecture.md §12).
- The recent list rests on a reading that waits for the owner, assumption A38 of docs/product-spec.md §16. The other reading of his answer is a Watch with no list at all.
- If watch delivery is unreliable, recordings arrive late or need a retry. The durable outbox and re-send mean nothing is lost. SP-2 measures delivery on the Ultra 2 early.
- The watch link acknowledges only a file that matches its checksum. After a third mismatch the phone keeps the damaged copy as a failed capture, and the watch keeps its own.
- A watch away from its phone keeps its recordings until the phone returns. It warns when little space is left and refuses new recordings at the limit (docs/product-spec.md §4.4).
- The cut order. If the build has to shrink, the complication families beyond one and the Smart Stack widget leave in its second step. One complication stays. Scope leaves only on the owner's word, and no agent cuts scope. A part that is cut is not started.
