# M1 · Skeleton on four devices

You can type a line on the iPhone, iPad and Mac and find it in the stream after relaunch, and record on the Watch and see "Saved" there. Local only, in the system's default look. The Watch does not deliver to the iPhone yet (that is M3), and it plays nothing back.

About 15 minutes, in two sittings. [How to use a checklist](README.md#how-to-use-a-checklist).

## Sitting 1 · Retire the old app and install

The build is signed for development as `com.p3ob7o.knot`, the retired app's identifier, so on a device that had the retired app it installs over it. So the order matters. Rows 01 to 09 come first, before the M1 milestone build is installed on a device that had the retired app: once it is replaced, its Recovery window cannot be opened there. Tell the orchestrator when they are done, or that the retired app is on none of your devices. The install follows on your word. Rows 10 to 16 start when `scripts/install.sh` has reported success.

The Mac rows of M1 to M3 use the **build Mac**: its notes are disposable until the M4 milestone build, and your other Mac gets its first Knot install at M4, with its own three rows for the retired app.

Rows 01 to 09 come in threes, one three for each device that had the retired Knot (0.2.0): the build Mac, the iPhone and the iPad. Mark a device's rows n/a if the retired app was never on it.
