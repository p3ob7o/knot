# M6 · Designed

You see the Claude Design handoff applied on all four devices, with the accessibility and speed targets met.

About 90 minutes, in three sittings. [How to use a checklist](README.md#how-to-use-a-checklist). Sitting 1 repeats the core rows of the M2 to M5 checklists on the designed build; the row each one repeats is named in brackets.

Start when `scripts/install.sh` has reported success for the M6 milestone build on the iPhone, the iPad mini, the Watch and your other Mac. The Mac rows use your other Mac, never the build Mac.

## Sitting 3 · Speed and sign-off

Each timed row takes 20 runs. Film the device from the side with another camera at 60 fps or more, and give the video to the orchestrator, who writes the median and the 95th percentile in Notes. Cold means after a restart of the device; warm means the app was open in the last minute.

| # | Device | Do | Expect | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
