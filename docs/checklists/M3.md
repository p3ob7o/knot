# M3 · Watch voice

You can tap the complication, record with the wrist down, and find the note transcribed on the iPhone, including after the phone was away.

About 30 minutes, in two sittings. [How to use a checklist](README.md#how-to-use-a-checklist).

## Sitting 1 · Recording and delivery

Start when `scripts/install.sh` has reported success for the M3 milestone build on the iPhone and the Watch. M3 has no Mac rows; any Mac step, such as giving a file to the orchestrator, uses the build Mac. Put Knot's complication on the watch face, add its Smart Stack widget and its Control Center control, and have the [read-aloud script](G4-read-aloud.md) open.

| M3.1-01 | Watch | Tap the Knot complication and speak for 20 seconds, lowering your wrist after the first few words. Raise it and stop. | A haptic and the timer when the microphone is live. The recording carries on with the wrist down, and ends with "Saved". | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.1-02 | Watch, iPhone nearby | Keep the iPhone unlocked with Knot open, and watch both screens. | The recording's row on the Watch changes to sent, and the note appears on the iPhone with its text, cleaned. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.1-03 | Watch, iPhone nearby | Swipe Knot away on the iPhone and lock the phone; keep it beside you. Record 30 seconds on the Watch (count slowly to thirty), stop, and start a stopwatch. ## Sitting 2 · Cues, privacy and failures

| # | Device | Do | Expect | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3.2-01 | Watch | Tap the complication. | Recording started: a haptic, and the timer starts. Nothing suggests recording before the microphone is live. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-02 | Watch | Say a sentence and stop. | Stopped and saved: a haptic and "Saved". | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-03 | Watch | Turn off Knot's microphone access in the Watch's Settings and tap the complication. Turn access back on afterwards. | Could not start: a failure haptic, and the reason when you raise your wrist. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-04 | Watch | Start a recording and stop it within a second. | Discarded: a light haptic, the Watch says nothing was kept, and nothing is listed. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-05 | Watch | Start a recording and count aloud. Have someone phone you. | Stopped by a call: the stop cue and the reason. Everything counted before the call is kept and reaches the iPhone. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-06 | Watch, iPhone nearby | Record a sentence and watch the list. | Delivered: the recording's row changes to sent. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-07 | Watch | After several notes have been transcribed on the iPhone, look through every Knot screen on the Watch, its complication, its Smart Stack widget and its control. | No text of any note or recording appears anywhere on the Watch: only times, lengths and delivery states. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
| M3.2-08 | Watch | Turn VoiceOver on. Tap the complication, say a sentence, and stop. Turn VoiceOver off. | VoiceOver announces the start and the stop and stays silent while recording. It reads no text. | ☐ pass ☐ fail | |
