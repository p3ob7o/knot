# Read-aloud script

Gate G4, wave 0. About 15 minutes.

You read ten short passages twice: once into the iPhone, once into the Watch. Knot's speech tests compare what the engine hears with the text below.

## Before you start

- Use Apple's **Voice Memos**. Leave its settings as they are.
- Find a quiet room.
- **iPhone:** hold it as you would for a voice note.
- **Watch:** raise your wrist and speak towards it, about a forearm away.
- Read each passage as written, at your normal pace.
- Don't read the passage number aloud.
- In passage 10, read the "um", "uh" and "like" as written.
- If you stumble badly, delete that recording and make it again. A small slip is fine.

## Naming the recordings

Each passage below gives two names. Give each recording its name, without `.m4a`: in Voice Memos, tap the recording's title and type the name.

- **iPhone:** rename each recording straight after you make it.
- **Watch:** do all ten on the Watch, then rename them on the iPhone once they appear there. They are the newest ones, in the order you made them.

Do the iPhone round first, then the Watch round.
