# SP-2 sitting: the Watch and the iPhone

For the owner, at gate G3, with the Apple Watch Ultra 2 and the iPhone. It starts once the spike app is installed on both (see README.md, "Build for the devices"). About 25 minutes of rows, plus one 30-minute recording that can run while SP-1's rows are done on the iPhone.

Everything the rows measure is written by the apps. At the end you share one CSV from the iPhone (row 12), and the report is written from it. Nothing here needs a stopwatch except row 2's optional screen recording.

What you say into the Watch is transcribed on the iPhone to time it. The spike keeps no transcript: it stores only the number of words. Say anything you like, but nothing you mind being in a recording on the two devices until you delete the app.

The Watch app has two pages. Swipe up for the second page, "SP-2 transfers", which holds the transfer controls and their numbers. The first page is the record control and nothing else, as in Knot.

| 8 | **Siri, 2 min.** Say "Record with Knot SP-2". Stop after 5 seconds. Then say "Record quietly with Knot SP-2". | First: the app comes forward recording, source "siri". | 10 | **iPhone app not running, phone locked, 4 min.** On the iPhone, close "Knot SP-2" (swipe it away) and lock the phone. On the Watch, record for 30 seconds with the button, stop. Its line "transcribed in … from background" and "stop → text …" give the 60-second target. "Background time granted" lists how long the system let the app run. ## If something goes wrong

- The iPhone says "watch app no": open the Watch app on the iPhone, find "Knot SP-2" under available apps and install it, or install it with `Spikes/SP-2/build.sh device` and `SP2_WATCH` set.
- A row's recording never shows on the iPhone: the Watch's page "Waiting" counts it. "Send unacknowledged again" sends it once more.
- The Watch's numbers and the iPhone's differ: the iPhone's "Watch queue → …" lines come from the Watch's own reports, which travel separately and may come later.
- To start again: "Clear" on both apps.
