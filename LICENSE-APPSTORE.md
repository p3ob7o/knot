# Additional permission for app store distribution

> Draft for Paolo's approval. An agent wrote it, and it is not legal advice. A lawyer should read it before the first build that someone else can download from a store.

Knot is free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or, at your option, any later version. The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

This file adds one permission to that licence. Section 7 of the GPL lets a copyright holder add permissions, and anyone who passes Knot on may remove this one from their copy.

## Why

App stores deliver apps under their own terms. Apple's terms add usage rules and copy protection to every app, and those can be read as "further restrictions", which section 10 of the GPL forbids. Without this permission it would be unclear whether Knot may be distributed through the App Store or TestFlight at all, by its author or by anyone else.

## The permission

Additional permission under GNU GPL version 3 section 7:

If you convey Knot, or a work based on it, in object code form through an application store such as Apple's App Store or TestFlight, the store's standard terms of distribution may apply to the copies conveyed that way, even where those terms restrict what a recipient may do with those copies in ways that this License would otherwise not permit. This holds only if you also make the Corresponding Source of exactly that object code available to everyone, at no charge, under this License, from a place that the app or its store listing names.

This permission covers only the copies conveyed through the store. It does not limit anyone's rights in the source code, or in copies they get any other way.
